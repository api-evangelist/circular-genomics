# Circular Genomics

> Precision-neurology diagnostics company using brain-derived circular RNA (circRNA)
> as stable, blood-based biomarkers for psychiatric and neurological disease. Spun
> out of the University of New Mexico in 2021, now headquartered in San Diego at
> Lilly Gateway Labs. Builds CircPath, a molecular-intelligence platform that
> discovers, validates and translates circRNA signals into biological insight.

## APIs

Circular Genomics publishes no public API, SDK, webhook, or developer
documentation. There is no OpenAPI, AsyncAPI, GraphQL SDL, MCP server, or A2A
agent card on any of its hosts. Test ordering and results delivery for licensed
healthcare providers run through a login-gated portal at
https://mindlight.circulargenomics.com/, which is a white-labeled CrelioHealth
(Creliant Software Pvt. Ltd.) laboratory information system rather than a
first-party developer surface.

## Products

- [CircPath platform](https://www.circulargenomics.com/products): proprietary molecular-intelligence platform translating brain-derived circRNA into biological insight.
- [Discover 1600+](https://www.circulargenomics.com/products): whole-transcriptome circRNA sequencing across 1,600+ brain-derived circRNAs with pathway-level analysis, for academic researchers, biopharma and CROs.
- [AD Predict](https://www.circulargenomics.com/products): research assay for predicting Alzheimer's disease symptom onset.
- [AD Detect](https://www.circulargenomics.com/products): research assay for blood-based detection of Alzheimer's disease.
- [MindLight](https://mindlight.circulargenomics.com/): SSRI antidepressant response test for major depressive disorder, ordered through the provider portal.
